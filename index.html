Proof, inspect and repair OTF, TTF, WOFF and WOFF2 files, and edit the kerning of UFO sources. Parsing, shaping and rebuilding all happen right here in the tab, so licensed and unreleased fonts stay on your machine — nothing uploads.

Font Tester

Renders two fonts side by side all the way from 72 pt down to 8 pt, so you can read how their TrueType or PostScript hinting actually rasterizes. Variable axes and OpenType features stay switchable at every size.

Hinting renders in full on Windows, partly on Linux, and not at all on macOS — so proof it on Windows.

OpenType Features Proof

Shows what each GSUB/GPOS feature actually does, proofed on real words in the right language with the affected glyphs marked. Includes per-language locl inventories, feature combinations, and a mark/mkmk anchor explorer.

Range Proof

Checks coverage of 61 curated Unicode blocks: which assigned characters are missing, which codepoints the font maps beyond the standard, and what else is in the file — down to glyphs that no codepoint can reach.

OT Tables Compare & Patch

Lines up head, name, OS/2, STAT and avar from any number of fonts in one table and highlights what differs. Edit a value and download rebuilt binaries, or export the edits as a JSON patch for the CLI.

PANOSE Editor

Reads the ten PANOSE digits out of OS/2, spells out what each one classifies, and writes your changes back into the font. Batch-loads a whole family and exports the resulting scheme.

Groups Control

Edits the kerning groups and the kerning of a UFO: drag glyphs into groups, check that members share the key glyph's margin, see the pairs a group affects and adjust them in a live preview. Saves only the changed files back into the folder.
